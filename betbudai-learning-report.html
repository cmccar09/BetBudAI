Verdict
⚖ Is it still learning?
Partially. The pipeline runs reliably, and data volume is excellent — 15,801 learning picks give a real corpus. But the learning loop has a break in it: signals are scored at pick-time but their individual outcomes are not being tracked back. This means the model has no ground truth for whether C&D winner, going match, or steam actually fired on a given pick — the three signals we claim are most valuable.

The score analysis confirms this: average score for wins (102.3) and losses (101.0) are nearly identical. A working learning system would gradually separate these distributions. They're flat. The model is not getting meaningfully smarter week to week on available evidence.
